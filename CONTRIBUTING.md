# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep the list useful.

## What belongs here

A tool, guide or technique that keeps AI assistants' accounts, memory, settings or history separate between work, clients and personal use. It must be:

- **Real and working:** it exists today and does what it says.
- **Public:** source or docs anyone can read. Closed tools are fine if they have public docs.
- **About separation:** a general AI tool doesn't belong unless separating contexts is its point.

## How to add an entry

1. Add one line to the right section, in this form:

   ```
   - [Name](https://link) - What it separates and how, in one or two sentences.
   ```

2. Keep the list in each section alphabetical, after any entry the section opens with.
3. One entry per pull request, with a short note on why it belongs.

## Style

- Start the description with a capital letter and end it with a full stop.
- Say what the tool does, not how good it is: no "best", "amazing" or "awesome".
- Name the platforms it runs on when that matters.
